## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-touch, editorial luxury aesthetic tailored specifically for Indonesian wedding stationery management and presentation. Combining the intimacy and grace of ceremonial print design with the systematic efficiency of modern SaaS inventory curation, the interface emphasizes order, prestige, and tactile delicacy.

The visual direction draws from **Minimalism** enriched with **Warm Luxury Accents**. Pristine white canvases and gossamer-soft neutral backdrops allow stationery proofs, foil-stamped card mockups, and typography samples to command primary visual weight. Interfaces avoid clutter, relying on deliberate typographic contrasts, fine hairlines, and warm amber-gold highlights to evoke craftsmanship, celebration, and enterprise clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Prestigious & Ceremonial:** Evokes the emotional significance of wedding announcements through refined serifs and curated metal tones.
- **Systematic & Clear:** Delivers immediate catalog clarity to printers, wedding planners, and administrative coordinators through structured cards and disciplined tier tagging.
- **Tactile Distinction:** Pure card assets pop against subtly elevated neutral backplates with definitive, crisp hairline boundaries.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly in **light mode** to honor the classic physical medium of paper stationery, letterpress, and bespoke foil stamping.

### Primary & Accent Roles
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#F57C00`):** Warm vibrant orange, applied selectively to primary interaction drivers, active category filters, and vital operational confirmations.
- **Secondary Accent (`#B45309`):** Deep amber bronze, deployed for bespoke luxury markers, administrative crests, and subtle gold ornamentation.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D97706`):** Luminous amber, used for interactive focus highlights and contextual status callouts.

### Surfaces & Neutral Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure pristine white dedicated to main catalog canvases and stationery presentation viewports.
- **Subtle Surface Tint (`#FAFAFA`):** Low-saturation canvas wash for application backdrops, control sidebars, and structural panels.
- **Borders & Dividers:** High-precision hairline borders (`#E5E7EB` for interactive bounds; `#F3F4F6` for internal list dividing lines) ensure white and cream wedding mockups do not bleed into the canvas.
- **Typography Tokens:** Primary text uses deep charcoal `#111827` to avoid harsh pure black; secondary text uses `#4B5563`; tertiary metadata relies on `#9CA3AF`.

### Category Segment Tier Rules
In strict observance of catalog policies, no Rupiah/IDR monetary amounts may appear on display cards. Tier badges convey categorization:
- **Premium:** Background `#FEF3C7`, Label `#92400E`, Border `#FDE68A`.
- **Menengah:** Background `#EFF6FF`, Label `#1E40AF`, Border `#BFDBFE`.
- **Ekonomis:** Background `#ECFDF5`, Label `#065F46`, Border `#A7F3D0`.
- **Super Hemat:** Background `#F3E8FF`, Label `#6B21A8`, Border `#E9D5FF`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between classical Indonesian wedding stationery aesthetics and operational platform precision:

- **Headings & Editorial Display (`Playfair Display`):** Used for platform page titles, catalog model titles (e.g., *Seri Rinjani*, *Bungur Royale*), collection headers, and promotional modal titles. Its high stroke contrast reflects letterpress and hot-foil craftsmanship.
- **Body, UI, & Data Elements (`Inter`):** Deployed across all functional interfaces, form fields, filter bars, breadcrumbs, status tables, specifications, and SKU numbers.
- **Case Conventions:** Category tier badges and metadata tags must be rendered in `label-sm` or `label-md` with uppercase transformation and deliberate letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) for elevated editorial crispness.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid system bounded by maximum container constraints to ensure gallery previews retain balanced proportions across wide desktop monitors.

### Breakpoints & Layout Model
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem` to `3rem`. Standard catalog cards sit in a 3 or 4-column layout (`col-span-4` or `col-span-3`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Catalog reflows to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1rem`. Catalog cards display in single-column stacks or high-density 2-column grids with compressed vertical padding.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing adheres to a strict 4px/8px incremental base. Internal card padding is standardized to `space-md` (`1rem`) for compact previews and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) for editorial detail sheets. Section headers maintain a consistent vertical baseline separation of `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) above card grids.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the tactile nature of pristine white paper goods without visual murkiness, elevation avoids dark, heavy dropshadows. Visual separation is accomplished using **fine structural outlines combined with ambient, warm-tinted micro-shadows**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Foundation (`#FAFAFA`):** Neutral canvas level. Zero elevation.
2. **Elevated Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity foreground. Outlined by default with `#E5E7EB` (1px solid) to cleanly delineate white invitation prints from light canvas backdrops.
3. **Ambient Card Rest State:** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`.
4. **Interactive Hover State:** Cards elevate smoothly with a vertical displacement of -2px to -4px: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`. Note the amber-tinged ambient base reflecting the secondary luxury tone.
5. **Overlays & Modals:** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` surrounded by a 1px boundary of `#E5E7EB`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined radius scale to balance modern software cleanliness with the soft cut edges of invitation stock:

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, checkboxes, notification toasts, and standard buttons.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Standard for catalog product cards, preview sheets, contextual dropdown flyouts, and modal panels.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for hero invitation showcase elements and floating operational docks.
- **Full Radius (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Dedicated strictly to pill-shaped horizontal filter chips, series navigation tabs, price-segment category badges, and circular thumbnail avatars.

## Components

### 1. Catalog Cards
- **Architecture:** White surface (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px solid `#E5E7EB` border and `rounded-lg` radius.
- **Image Showcase Container:** Top aspect ratio 4:3 or 1:1, set inside a neutral framing bay (`#FAFAFA`) to accentuate white card stock. 
- **Hover Action Overlay:** On hover, a soft scrim (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.35)`) manifests with smooth transition, unveiling quick-action pill buttons ("Lihat Detail", "Unduh Mockup", "Edit Data") utilizing backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm`).
- **Card Content Area:** Title in `title-lg` (`Playfair Display`), metadata (envelope type, paper spec) in `body-sm`, and tier tag anchored top-right or bottom-left.

### 2. Segment Tier Badges
- **Strict Rule:** Currency marks (`Rp`, `IDR`, or nominal figures) are prohibited.
- **Form:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, border width 1px, typography `label-sm`.
- **Variants:**
  - *Premium:* Background `#FEF3C7`, Text `#92400E`, Border `#FDE68A`.
  - *Menengah:* Background `#EFF6FF`, Text `#1E40AF`, Border `#BFDBFE`.
  - *Ekonomis:* Background `#ECFDF5`, Text `#065F46`, Border `#A7F3D0`.
  - *Super Hemat:* Background `#F3E8FF`, Text `#6B21A8`, Border `#E9D5FF`.

### 3. Filter Tabs (Series & Collections)
- **Geometry:** Horizontal scrollable rail of pill-shaped buttons (`rounded-full`).
- **Unselected:** Transparent or `#FFFFFF` background, text `#4B5563`, border 1px solid `#E5E7EB`.
- **Active:** Background `#F57C00`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#F57C00`, accompanied by a subtle ambient glow (`0 2px 8px rgba(245, 124, 0, 0.25)`).

### 4. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#F57C00`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, radius `rounded-md`. Hover states shift to `#E65100`.
- **Luxury / Accent:** Background `#B45309`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover shifts to `#92400E`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, text `#111827`. Hover shifts to background `#FAFAFA` with border `#D1D5DB`.

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Styling:** Height 42px, radius `rounded-md`, border 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, background `#FFFFFF`, font `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#F57C00` with an outer focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 124, 0, 0.15)`).
- **Search Prepend:** Clean monochrome magnifying icon tinted `#9CA3AF`.

### 6. Catalog Data Table
- **Container:** Border 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, radius `rounded-lg`, overflow hidden.
- **Header:** Background `#FAFAFA`, text `#4B5563`, font `label-md`, uppercase, border-bottom 1px solid `#E5E7EB`.
- **Rows:** Alternating hover highlight (`#FAFAFA`), divider lines 1px solid `#F3F4F6`, vertical height 56px with aligned thumbnail previews.

### 7. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square with `rounded-sm` corners. Checked state fills with `#F57C00` displaying a crisp white glyph.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric ring design, checking with an outer accent border `#F57C00` and centered `#F57C00` pip.
